## Scope

Visitor mode: Persuade. Public marketing landing page, unauthenticated. Audience: the renter named in PRODUCT.md — about to sign a residential lease they have no leverage to negotiate.

Job: understand in seconds what Redline does, see the one mechanism it can prove (a lease sentence becoming a ranked, cited flag), and take the one action offered.

Action/task: a single primary action — try it on a document. No pricing, no account creation copy, no feature tour beyond the one demonstrated mechanism.

Proof/content: the demonstration is synthetic but realistic lease text, clearly the product's own working material, not a real customer's document. No invented prices, customers, testimonials, or quotes (PRODUCT.md Evidence on Hand; user's explicit instruction).

Constraints: claims nothing PRD.md excludes — no verdict on whether to sign, no legal-advice framing, no scanned/photographed-document support, no document types beyond what v1 serves (residential leases only). Real product name "Redline." Standing not-legal-advice disclaimer must appear.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page owns one idea — a lease sentence turning into a numbered, wired violation tag citing the exact clause it broke — and refuses the SaaS-dashboard-screenshot-in-browser-chrome arrangement every AI contract-review competitor already ships.

OWN-WORLD: A building inspector's clipboard world: kraft/manila tag stock, carbon-numbered stamps, wired red tags, graphite ink line work on paper white. Committed color strategy — one violation-red (#C4362A) carries 30-60% of the surface via stamps, wire ties, and tag rules; kraft (#E8DCC0), paper white (#F6F1E4), and graphite ink (#2B2B28) restrain the rest, with muted amber (#D97F2E) marking push-on flags and dashed graphite marking clarify flags. Type: Big Shoulders Stencil for tag headers and the wordmark treatment, Courier Prime for ticket/citation numbers and dollar figures, Public Sans for body copy and quoted clause text.

STORY: A renter arrives already uneasy about a document they can't negotiate. The hero proves in one glance that Redline turns their exact lease text into cited, ranked findings — never a verdict on whether to sign. They scroll through the mechanism once, see the standing disclaimer, and take the one action offered: try it on a document.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A lease paragraph sits at left as plain paper; a numbered red violation tag wires onto its flagged sentence at right, the sentence quoted verbatim inside the tag in Public Sans, its action bucket stamped in Courier Prime, dollar-impact tags carrying a stamped circular amount. Below: the single primary action, "Try it on a document," and the standing not-legal-advice disclaimer set in the same stamp register.

FORM: IMPECCABLE'S PICK, "The Code-Violation Tag" (building-inspection red tags), chosen over the assigned roll "The Citation Counter" (a municipal intake-counter world) in a direction-scope round; seed key 602b2a5d. Raised with: literal quotation-set citations (donated by the declined Industrial Quote Grammar challenger), stamped dollar-amount callouts (donated by the declined Primary Toy Catalogue challenger), per-bucket mark permanence — solid stamp for remove/modify, confident marker for push-on, dashed pencil for clarify (donated by the declined Floated Pigment Bath challenger), and a considered sequential reveal as tags wire on in ranked order (donated by the declined Sleeping City Fold challenger).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
